Sue Ann Staff Estate Winery Wedding | Aldona & Tyler

Aldona and Tyler's early October wedding at Sue-Ann Staff Estate Winery was a masterclass in elegant, early autumn vibes. What made this day extra special was the journey. Aldona and Tyler traveled all the way from Australia to celebrate their marriage in the heart of the Niagara wine country. It was an unusually warm fall day which was perfect for their outdoor celebration.

Getting Ready at Inn on the Twenty

The morning began at the stunning Inn on the Twenty in Jordan. I joined Aldona and her bridesmaids in one of the suites at Inn on the Twenty. Aldona was one of the calmest, most radiant brides I've ever photographed. She looked like she came straight out of a bridal magazine. After capturing some timeless portraits with her bridesmaids and family, a school bus arrived to whisk the party over to the winery.

Outdoor Ceremony at Sue-Ann Staff Estate

The ceremony site at Sue-Ann Staff is hard to beat, offering a stunning view over the water. The decor was perfectly curated with a crisp white theme that popped against the lush vineyard greenery. I met Tyler and his groomsmen before the ceremony and they were in great spirits. The ceremony started and after everyone made their way down the aisle, Aldona and her father arrived in a horse-drawn carriage. They had a beautiful ceremony and once it was finished we began taking family and wedding party photos.

Portraits at Sue-Ann Winery

After that we focused on photographing Aldona and Tyler. With two horses hanging out on the property we used them for some photos as well and I love how those images turned out! Aldona and Tyler were so easy to take photos of. The chemistry they have together is magical and I could feel the love they had for each other throughout the day.

Wedding Reception

We then headed into the tent for their outdoor reception. They had an amazing choreographed first dance while the band played. The rest of the evening was filled with great music, thoughtful speeches, awesome dancing and fantastic food. The party went all night and I think Aldona and Tyler were smiling the entire night celebrating with their family and friends.

Congratulations you two!
